## What are PostQode Workflows?

PostQode Workflows are structured guides that automate or standardize multi-step processes. They help you:

-   **Automate Repetitive Tasks:** Streamline common development activities, such as creating tests or reviewing code.
-   **Enforce Best Practices:** Ensure that every team member follows a consistent, high-quality process.
-   **Improve Efficiency:** Reduce the time and effort required to complete complex tasks.
-   **Share Knowledge:** Document and share your team's best practices and standard operating procedures.

## How to Use Workflows

To use a workflow, simply invoke it by name in your chat with PostQode. The workflow will guide you through the process, asking for any required inputs and providing a structured output.

## Creating Your Own Workflows

You can create your own workflows to automate any process that is specific to your team or project. A good workflow should include:

1.  **A Clear Objective:** What the workflow aims to achieve.
2.  **Required Inputs:** Any information the workflow needs to get started.
3.  **A Step-by-Step Process:** A clear, logical sequence of steps.
4.  **Actionable Outputs:** The final result or deliverable of the workflow.
